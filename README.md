## 📖 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales, order fulfillment, product popularity, and revenue performance for **Verdant (FNP Data Analytics Case Study)** using **Advanced Microsoft Excel**. By combining multi-table relational data modeling, custom calculated columns, dynamic Pivot Tables, and interactive dashboard visuals, this project turns transactional e-commerce records into executive-level business intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Evaluate overall business growth through key performance indicators (Total Revenue, Total Orders, Units Sold).
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior across various categories, occasions (Diwali, Valentine's Day, etc.), and geographical locations.
- Monitor order fulfillment efficiency by calculating order-to-delivery lead times (`Diff_Order_Delivery`).
- Identify top-performing product categories (Cakes, Flowers, Personalized Gifts, Sweets, Plants, etc.).
- Build an interactive, executive-facing Excel dashboard using dynamic Slicers and Pivot Charts for quick decision-making.

---

## 🛠 Excel Functions & Analytics Techniques Used

### Data Cleaning & Modeling
- **Data Structuring:** Multi-sheet transactional architecture (`Customers`, `Orders`, `Products`, `Pivot Tables`, `Executive Dashboard`).
- **Data Transformation:** Standardized dates, timestamps, and customer ID mappings across tables.

### Formulas & Calculated Columns
- **Date & Time Intelligence:** `YEAR()`, `MONTH()`, `TEXT()`, `HOUR()`, `DAYNAME()` for temporal sales patterns.
- **Delivery Lead-Time Calculation:** Calculated date differences between `Order_Date` and `Delivery_Date`.
- **Lookup & Reference Functions:** `XLOOKUP` / `VLOOKUP` to fetch product pricing, categories, and customer attributes.
- **Conditional Logic:** `IF`, `IFS`, `AND`, `OR` for segmentation and status flagging.
- **Aggregation Functions:** `SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS` for custom summarized reporting.

### Advanced Excel Tools & Visualization
- **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts:** Aggregated multi-dimensional data for monthly, category-wise, and location-wise sales.
- **Dynamic Slicers & Timelines:** Interactive filters connected across multiple Pivot Charts for instant data drill-down.
- **Executive Dashboard Design:** Clean visual layout incorporating KPIs, bar charts, trend lines, and clear category breakdowns.

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Revenue & Sales Performance
- Total revenue generation across the entire timeline ($586,000+).
- Monthly revenue trends highlighting peak sales months (Valentine's Day & Festive Seasons).
- Product-level and category-level revenue contributions.

### 2. Operational & Fulfillment Analysis
- Average order-to-delivery time calculation (Fulfillment SLA analysis).
- Hourly order distribution to identify peak traffic windows.
- Day-wise sales distribution to analyze weekend vs. weekday shopping behavior.

### 3. Customer & Occasion Analytics
- Occasion-based sales distribution (Diwali, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, Anniversaries).
- Top revenue-generating cities and geographic hubs.
- Customer purchase frequency and high-value customer identification.

E-commerce businesses process thousands of orders daily. This Excel dashboard provides stakeholders with immediate answers for:
- **Executive Decision Making:** Quick high-level view of revenue KPIs and monthly trends.
